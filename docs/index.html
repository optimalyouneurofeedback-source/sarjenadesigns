What I make

Sarjena Designs creates organically designed digital miniature and full size wallpaper, book covers and art prints.

The studio

I create from the unknown — original designs drawn from nature, from the places I visit, and from what rises from within.

Each design begins as a question I can't yet answer. I follow it wherever it leads — into a leaf, into a story, into a small, quiet room — and shape it slowly, until the idea finds its form.
